Two market-data paths, one fixed dashboard

OpenStock already uses TradingView for rich visual market surfaces and Finnhub for app-owned stock actions. The gap is that those paths do not share a canonical instrument, quote-freshness, or alert contract.

Provider-managed

TradingView is already in the product

Application gap

Freshness and alerts are separate

OpenStock does not receive a normalized quote stream from the embeds. Its scheduled alert path still assumes Finnhub stock symbols and has no shared freshness contract.
